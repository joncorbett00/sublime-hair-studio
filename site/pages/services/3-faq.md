#> Before you book

# Questions we get
# every single week

### How long does creative colour actually take?
Between four and seven hours, and occasionally two appointments. If your photo shows
pastel over dark hair, it is almost certainly two. We would rather tell you that now
than at hour five.

### Do I need a patch test?
Yes, for any colour service, and it has to be at least 48 hours before. It takes two
minutes at the front desk — swing by, or book it alongside a fringe trim.

### I used box dye. Will you still see me?
Yes. Tell us when you book so we can put enough time aside, and bring the box if you
still have it. The ingredients list genuinely helps.

### What is your cancellation policy?
Twenty-four hours' notice, no charge, and any deposit comes back. Under that, or a
no-show, and we keep the deposit. We're a small studio — a lost four-hour slot is
a real loss. Emergencies are emergencies; just tell us.

### Can I bring a photo?
Please do. Bring several, including ones you dislike — knowing what you do not want is
often more useful than knowing what you do.

### Do you cut kids' hair?
Yes, from about age five, and we charge less for it. Under five is usually better at a
place set up for it, and we will happily point you at one.

### Can I really get a cut on my lunch break?
Yes. Book The Lunch-Hour Cut: 45 minutes, starting from noon, for a repeat of a shape
you already like. If you want something new, book a full cut so there's time to talk.

### Are you accessible?
The studio is on the second floor, up one flight of stairs. If stairs are hard for you,
call us before booking and we'll talk through what works.
