#> Planning a wedding

# Questions couples
# ask us first

### Do I really need a trial?
For a wedding, yes. It's where we try the style with your veil or accessories, check how it
photographs, and agree on the plan, so the morning itself has no surprises. Book it as an
Up-Do & Event Styling appointment, ideally four to eight weeks before the day.

### How far ahead should we book the wedding morning?
As early as you know the date. Saturdays from May to October go first — six months ahead is
comfortable, and a year is not unusual.

### How many people can you do in one morning?
Up to six, in the studio. For a bigger party, ask us and we'll work out whether a second
stylist or an earlier start makes it possible.

### Do you come to the venue?
Not at the moment — everyone comes to the studio, which is a two-minute walk from Elgin and
a much calmer place to get ready than most hotel rooms.

### How does pricing and the deposit work?
Up-dos start at $110 each and the trial is booked at the same rate. The bridal party is
quoted per party, and we take a deposit to hold the date, which comes off the final bill.

### How do we get a quote for the party?
Call the studio at (613) 567-7400 or email hello@sublimehair.ca with the date, the number
of people and a few photos of what you like.
