#> The studio

# Upstairs, and
# cozy [on purpose]{accent}

Street level on Elgin is busy. Up our stairs it isn't. The studio sits on a second
floor in the Golden Triangle — a warm room with good light, a few chairs, and music
you can talk over.

It's a small space, so no one is shouting across a busy floor, and your stylist
isn't juggling three clients at once. Neighbours have been climbing these stairs
for over twenty years; plenty of them still do.
