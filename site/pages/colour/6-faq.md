#> Before you book colour

# Questions we get
# every single week

### How long does creative colour actually take?
Between four and seven hours, and occasionally two appointments. If your photo shows
pastel over dark hair, it is almost certainly two. We would rather tell you that now
than at hour five.

### Do I need a patch test?
Yes, for any colour service, and it has to be at least 48 hours before. It takes two
minutes at the front desk — swing by, or book it alongside a fringe trim.

### I used box dye. Will you still see me?
Yes. Tell us when you book so we can put enough time aside, and bring the box if you
still have it. The ingredients list genuinely helps.

### How long will vivids last?
Pastels soften in a few weeks; deeper jewel tones hold longer. Wash cool, wash less, use the
colour-safe shampoo we recommend, and book a gloss between bigger appointments to keep it bright.

### Balayage or highlights?
Balayage is painted by hand and grows out softly, so you come back in months, not weeks.
Foils give a brighter, more even result that needs a closer eye on the regrowth. We'll tell
you which suits your hair at the consult.

### Is there a deposit?
Yes, for creative colour and balayage: it holds a long appointment, comes back with 24 hours'
notice, and comes off the final bill.
