# [Accessibility]{accent}

We want everyone to be able to browse the racks and find something good. If something gets
in your way — at the boutique or on this website — tell us and we'll work it out with you.

## Getting to the boutique

The boutique is on the second floor at 190 MacLaren Street, **up one flight of stairs**,
in the same studio as Sublime Hair Studio. If stairs are hard for you, call us at
(613) 555-0142 before you come and we'll work out what suits you — we can bring pieces
down to you, or send photos and measurements first so you know what's worth the trip.

Street parking is on MacLaren and Gilmour, and there's a Green P a two-minute walk away on
Cooper. Buses along Bank and Elgin stop close by.

## In the boutique

- **Service animals and support persons** are always welcome.
- **Ask for help** reaching a rack, reading a label or a size, or finding the fitting room.
- **Measurements on request.** Vintage sizing runs differently — we're happy to measure a piece for you, in the boutique or by email.

## This website

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. The site
is built to:

- work with a keyboard, a screen reader, and zoom up to 200%;
- describe every piece in words, not just in photos;
- keep text readable, with strong contrast;
- tone down movement when your device asks for reduced motion.

## Other formats

Any information on this site is available in another format on request, such as large
print or read over the phone.
