#> How to shop Bijou

# Come for one piece,
# leave with [three]{accent}

The website shows a few key finds. The shop is where the rest is — rack after rack of it,
sorted by what it is rather than where it came from. Most people come in for something
they saw online and find something else on the way to the fitting room.

[See the latest finds](/finds)

### 01
#### Look online
The finds here are a taste of what's in, and the newest arrivals.

### 02
#### Come in
Tuesday to Saturday, one flight up on MacLaren. Browse the racks as long as you like.

### 03
#### Try it on
One fitting room, a long mirror, and no rush.

### 04
#### Ask us anything
About any piece — we'll tell you the label, the era and anything that was altered.
