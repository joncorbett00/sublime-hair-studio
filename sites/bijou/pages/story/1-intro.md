#> Our story

# Good clothes deserve
# a [second life]{accent}

Bijou started with a simple idea: that the best clothes in the city are often the ones
already made — a coat too good to leave behind at an estate sale, a suit that fits like it
was cut for you, a dress nobody else will be wearing.

Now we go looking all year — estate sales, collectors, closets being cleared — and bring
back the pieces worth wearing again: vintage with real character, designer labels at a fair
price, and the good everyday things you'll wear until they wear out.
