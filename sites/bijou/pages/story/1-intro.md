#> Our story

# Montréal plays
# every city but [itself]{accent}

It has been Manhattan in the seventies, Paris in the twenties and a Boston that never
quite existed. Every one of those productions builds a wardrobe — coats bought in threes
so they can age on screen, gowns beaded by hand for a single number, suits let out half an
inch for a scene shot sitting down — and when the shoot wraps, most of it is sold off by
the rack.

Bijou started with one of those racks. Now we go to every wrap sale we can, pick the
pieces worth wearing again, and bring them to the shop in Ottawa: coats, suiting,
dresses, knitwear and accessories, each still carrying its wardrobe tag.
