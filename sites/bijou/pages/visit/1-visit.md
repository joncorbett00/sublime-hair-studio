### Finding us
![](lu-door-open)
The street door at 190 MacLaren, then one flight up to the second floor. The boutique is in
the same studio as Sublime Hair Studio — just ask for Bijou.

### Parking
![](lu-circle-parking)
Street parking on MacLaren and Gilmour, metered until 5:30pm. There's a Green P a
two-minute walk away on Cooper.

### Getting here
![](lu-bus)
About two minutes from Elgin, and roughly twelve minutes on foot from Parliament Station.
Buses along Bank and Elgin stop close by.

### Ask about a piece
![](lu-mail)
Every find on the site has an "Ask about this piece" button. It writes to us with the
piece's name, so we know which one you mean.

### Looking for something?
![](lu-search)
Most of the shop isn't online. Tell us what you're after — a size, an era, a label —
and we'll look through the racks.

### Holds
![](lu-clock)
We can hold a piece for a day or two. Write to us with its name and when you can come in.

### Accessibility
![](lu-accessibility)
The boutique is on the second floor, up one flight of stairs. If stairs are hard for you,
call before you come and we'll work out what suits you.
