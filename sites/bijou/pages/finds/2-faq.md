#> Before you come in

# Good to
# [know]{accent}

### Can I buy online?
No — everything is sold in the boutique. Most pieces need trying on, and every one is one
of one. Write to us about anything you see here and we'll tell you if it's still in.

### Is this everything you have?
Not even close. We only put a handful of key finds online. The shop holds much more —
outerwear, suiting, dresses, knitwear and accessories from productions shot all over the city.

### A piece I saw here is gone. Is it sold?
Not necessarily. The site shows our latest finds, so older ones come off it as new ones go
up. Write to us with its name and we'll check the racks.

### Can you hold something for me?
For a day or two, yes. Write to us with the piece's name and when you can come in.

### Is it really from the film?
Yes. Everything comes to us from a production's own wrap sale or wardrobe department, and
we tell you what we know: the production, who wore it and, where there was one, the scene
number from its wardrobe tag. Some productions don't let us name the actor, so we don't.

### Do you ship?
Not yet. Everything is sold in the boutique at 190 MacLaren Street.
