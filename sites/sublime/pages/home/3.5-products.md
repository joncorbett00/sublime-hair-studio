# Our Product Lines

Excellence resonates in the assortment of products available for purchase at Sublime Hair Studio. Our salon uses and carries only the highest-quality, performance-based hair products developed by experts from France and The Netherlands.

### René Furterer

#### Paris, France

Plant-based, research backed care for hair and scalp.

René Furterer is a luxury line designed specifically to improve the condition of your hair and scalp. Backed by pharmaceutical research and produced using the finest plant-based essential oils, it is at the top of its class for hair and scalp treatment.

Proven results, beautifully presented, and available in Ottawa only at Sublime.

### Layrite

#### Southern California, USA

Men's grooming pomades that "hold like wax and wash out like gel."

For over two decades, Layrite Men's Grooming Products have been setting the benchmark for traditional barbering and men's grooming around the world. Their water-soluble pomades "hold like wax and wash out like gel," says founder Donnie Hawley.

Whether your hair is straight, wavy, curly, coarse and thick or fine and thin, there's a shine from matte to super-shine and a hold for texture and volume. The name came from a regular who told Donnie the products "make hair lay right" and a brand was born.

[Layrite](https://layrite.com){target=_blank}

### Reuzel

#### Rotterdam, Netherlands

The Scumbag Barbers' pomades, made for pompadours, quiffs, flat-tops and fades.

Reuzel was formulated over two decades ago by the "Scumbag Barbers", Leen and Bertus, who needed a product worthy of their newly founded Schorem barbershop. What began as a line for their signature pompadours, quiffs, flat-tops and fades quickly became a global mainstay for barbers and clients alike.

The range covers wonderfully scented styling, shaving and beard products for every hair type. We carry most of the pomades, and anything else in the line can be special ordered.

[Reuzel](https://reuzel.com){target=_blank}

### Keune

#### Netherlands, since 1922

Heritage European haircare, from long-lasting colour to restorative treatments.

Keune is a heritage European brand devoted to the artistry of professional haircare. Founded in 1922, it blends scientific innovation with premium, ethically sourced ingredients, and each formula is crafted with precision, sustainability and care — from vibrant, long-lasting colour to restorative treatments for hair and scalp.

Exclusively available at Sublime, in salon or by special order.
