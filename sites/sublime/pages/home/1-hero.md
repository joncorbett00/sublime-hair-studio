# Welcome to Sublime,

Sublime Hair Studio is built on the idea that every client deserves a style that is the outcome of creativity and originality. Tucked in amongst the restaurants, shops, and small businesses that line Ottawa’s buzzing Elgin Street, Sublime is a small, private space that offers clients a warm welcome, attentive service, and unparalleled expertise.

Open for over two decades, Sublime has been executing modern and sophisticated hairstyles, transcending trends that Sonia and her team have consistently adapted to. Throughout the years, trust has remained at the center of the client experience. Whether it’s the product, cut, colour, or conversation, clients can leave Sublime knowing their needs have been the focus of every appointment.
