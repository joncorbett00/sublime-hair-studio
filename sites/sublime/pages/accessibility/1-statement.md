#> Everyone welcome

# [Accessibility]{accent}

We want everyone to be comfortable at Sublime, from finding us to leaving the chair. If
something gets in your way — at the studio or on this website — tell us and we'll work
it out with you.

## Getting to the studio

The studio is on the second floor at 190 MacLaren Street, **up one flight of stairs**.
If stairs are hard for you, call us at (613) 567-7400 before booking and we'll talk
through what works — timing, help on the stairs, or anything else we can arrange.

Street parking is on MacLaren and Gilmour, and there's a Green P a two-minute walk away on
Cooper. Buses along Bank and Elgin stop close by.

## In the studio

- **Service animals and support persons** are always welcome.
- **Tell us what you need** — a quieter time of day, a break during a long colour, a chair adjustment, music turned down, or written aftercare instead of spoken. Mention it when you book or when you arrive.
- **Sensitivities.** If you have allergies or skin sensitivities, let us know and we'll patch-test and choose products accordingly.

## This website

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. The site
is built to:

- work with a keyboard, a screen reader, and zoom up to 200%;
- describe photos with text alternatives;
- keep text readable, with strong contrast;
- tone down movement when your device asks for reduced motion — the video clips won't play by themselves.

Some things we know about:

- **Booking** happens on Square Appointments, which we don't control. If it gives you any trouble, call us and we'll book you in by phone.
- **Video clips** on the home page are short and have no speech; they show the work, and the same services are described in words on our Services and Colour pages.

## Other formats

Any information on this site — prices, aftercare, our cancellation policy — is available
in another format on request, such as large print or read over the phone.

## Tell us about a barrier

Call (613) 567-7400, email [hello@sublimehair.ca](mailto:hello@sublimehair.ca), or tell us
in person at the studio. Let us know what you were trying to do and what got in the way,
and we'll get back to you within a few business days.
