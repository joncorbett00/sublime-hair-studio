#> Before you book colour

# Questions we get every single week

### How long does creative colour actually take?

Between three and seven hours, and occasionally two appointments. If your photo shows pastel over dark hair, it is almost certainly two. We would rather tell you that now than at hour five.

### I used box dye. Will you still see me?

Yes. Tell us when you book so we can put enough time aside, and bring the box if you still have it. The ingredients list genuinely helps.

### How long will vivids last?

Pastels soften in a few weeks; deeper jewel tones hold longer. Wash cool, wash less, use a colour-safe shampoo — we keep Keune and René Furterer on the shelf, which almost nowhere else in Ottawa does — and book a gloss between bigger appointments to keep it bright.

### Balayage or highlights?

Balayage is painted by hand and grows out softly, so you come back in months, not weeks. Foils give a brighter, more even result that needs a closer eye on the regrowth. We'll tell you which suits your hair at the consult.

### Is there a deposit?

No deposit, but we take a credit card when you book. It is only charged — a $50 cancellation fee — if you cancel or reschedule within 48 hours of your appointment.
