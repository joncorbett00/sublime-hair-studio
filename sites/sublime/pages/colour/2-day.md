#> How a colour day goes

# Long, but
# never [a guess]{accent}

The plan is made at the consult, so on the day nobody is improvising with bleach. You'll know
how many sittings it takes, what it costs, and what it will look like in six weeks, before
anything is mixed.

[Book a colour consult](/book)

### 12:00
#### The plan, again
A quick look at the consult notes and your photos, including the ones you don't like.

### 12:30
#### Lightening
Slow and even, checked the whole way. This is the part that decides how bright the colour gets.

### 2:30
#### Colour
Placement, panels, shadow roots — painted section by section, the way it was planned.

### 4:15
#### Gloss, cut, done
A gloss to seal it, a cut to finish it, and aftercare that keeps it bright.
