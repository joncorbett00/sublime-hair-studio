level and come up one flight of stairs to the second floor, where it's quieter.
Close enough to the office for a lunch-hour cut, and just around the corner if you
live in the neighbourhood.

### Parking
![](lu-circle-parking)
Street parking on MacLaren and Gilmour, metered until 5:30pm. There is a Green P a
two-minute walk away on Cooper.

### From the office
![](lu-briefcase)
A short walk from most downtown offices — about two minutes from Elgin, and Parliament
Station is roughly twelve minutes on foot. Buses along Bank and Elgin stop close by.

### Accessibility
![](lu-accessibility)
The studio is on the second floor, up one flight of stairs. If stairs are hard for
you, call before booking and we will talk through what works.

### Bikes
![](lu-bike)
There are bike rings on MacLaren near the door. Lock up on the street and come on up.

### Lunch-hour cuts
![](lu-timer)
Book a 45-minute slot over lunch. We keep to the clock so you get back on time.

### On the shelf
![](lu-shopping-bag)
We carry René Furterer and Keune, which almost no one else in Ottawa does. Worth the climb
even if you are not booked in — come up and ask.

### Cancellations
![](lu-calendar-x)
We ask that you please reschedule or cancel before the beginning of your appointment.

Please cancel up to 48 hours in advance, otherwise you will be charged a cancellation fee
of $50.\*

\*Upon booking we require 1 credit card on file that will only be charged if you do not
cancel within 48 hours before your scheduled appointment.
