### Charlotte
#### Miniature golden doodle · Dec 2013 – Feb 2026

![Charlotte](/images/crew/charlotte.webp)

Upon entering Sublime, you'll be greeted by a friendly bark and tail wag from Charlotte, a
miniature golden doodle. Charlotte loves to spend her time between her shampoo chair and a
vintage hair dryer near her Momma, Sonia.

Although Charlotte keeps to herself while the team is busy at work, you may notice her
beautiful big brown eyes catching yours, along with the odd plea for a smooch or a pet as you
pass her. Offer her a treat and she'll love you forever.

For those who aren't really dog friendly, she may just win you over. With her golden locks
and calm demeanour, Charlotte brings a smile to nearly every client — even if you aren't much
of a dog person, you may just become a "Charlotte person" by the time you pet her on your way
out the door.

### Bijou
#### Miniature poodle · Charlotte's little brother

![Bijou](/images/crew/bijou.webp)

Upon entering Sublime, you'll be greeted by a cheerful bark and a wagging tail from Bijou,
our miniature poodle and beloved salon dog. Charlotte's younger brother, he is inquisitive,
calm and charming, and his gentle personality wins over every guest who walks through our
door.

You'll often find him lounging by the window, keeping a watchful eye on the neighbourhood, or
admiring your new style from a distance while he waits to welcome the next client.

Outside of the salon, Bijou enjoys strolls across the E.B. Eddy Bridge to Aylmer, exploring
the Glebe, and sampling new treat combinations.
