#> Our mission

# The focus
# is [on you]{accent}

Sublime Hair Studio is built on the idea that every client deserves a style that is the
outcome of creativity and originality. Tucked in amongst the restaurants, shops and small
businesses that line Ottawa's buzzing Elgin Street, Sublime is a small, private space that
offers a warm welcome, attentive service and unparalleled expertise.

Open for over a decade, Sublime has been executing modern and sophisticated styles,
transcending trends that Sonia and her team have consistently adapted to. Through the years,
trust has remained at the centre of the client experience. Whether it's the product, cut,
colour or conversation, clients leave knowing their needs were the focus of every
appointment.

*Nous parlons français.*
