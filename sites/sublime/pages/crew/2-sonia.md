Before the studio upstairs there were runways, shoots and a lot of hairspray. This is the
short version of how the precision got here.

[Book with Sonia](/book)

### 2001
#### Trained, and certified
Sonia trained in cutting and colour and earned her certification — then kept taking classes,
which she still does every year.

![A training floor, mirrors all the way down](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1536520002442-39764a41e987?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1000&h=800&q=80)

### 2005
#### Backstage at the shows
Runway seasons: a rail of looks, a queue of models and minutes per head, for hair that has to
hold under hot lights and look right from every seat.

![The rail backstage before a show](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1470309864661-68328b2cd0a5?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1000&h=800&q=80)

### 2009
#### In front of the camera
Editorial shoots and campaigns, where every strand is shown at full size and a wave has to
survive six hours of lighting changes.

![Editorial waves, shot for a campaign](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1512316609839-ce289d3eba0a?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1000&h=800&q=80)

### 2013
#### One flight up
She brought that precision home: a small studio up a flight of stairs just off Elgin, where
the same technique comes without the rush.

![The studio on the second floor](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1521590832167-7bcbfaa6381f?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1000&h=800&q=80)

### Today
#### Still behind her own chair
The hardest colour corrections in the shop, creative colour and curly cuts — and a team she
trained to work the same way.

![An up-do being pinned to last the night](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1582095133179-bfd08e2fc6b3?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1000&h=800&q=80)
