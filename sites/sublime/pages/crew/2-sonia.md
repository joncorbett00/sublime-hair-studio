Sublime Hair Studio is an outlet for Sonia's creative imagination and unique stylistic
approaches. She puts an incredible amount of dedication into creating a personalized,
memorable experience for every person who sits in her chair — and has built a reputation
for innovative cut and colour techniques along the way.

### 1980s
#### Punk, and an apprenticeship at thirteen
Sonia's career began with a passion for punk hairstyles. She started her apprenticeship at
the very young age of 13, among European hairstylists, and that is where she built her
foundation.

She went on to learn from the world's best academies and mentors — Vidal Sassoon, Toni & Guy
London, Schwarzkopf, Alain Larivée and Anthony Edge — and became a master hair cutter with a
genius for colour formulation.

![A training floor, mirrors all the way down](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1536520002442-39764a41e987?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1000&h=800&q=80)

### 2000s
#### Home from New York
In the early 2000s Sonia returned to Ottawa from New York City, freshly inspired by the
cutting-edge SoHo loft salons where the client experience came first. She envisioned a salon
with the same obsession for style and service — and rejected the notion that high-fashion
hair needs a "so-cool-it's-cold" room.

![A loft salon, all brick and big windows](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1470309864661-68328b2cd0a5?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1000&h=800&q=80)

### Sublime
#### A salon against the grain
Her skill, precision and experience became a studio that puts the individual first: every
look starts with an in-depth consultation, and her attention to her clients is matched only
by her attention to detail in the work.

![The studio, just off Elgin](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1521590832167-7bcbfaa6381f?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1000&h=800&q=80)

### Today
#### From barbering to alchemy
Sonia excels at men's hair and barbering, and at the other end of the spectrum she is a true
alchemist with colour. Sublime is a full-service salon — a destination for everyone.

Natural or dramatic colour, a sophisticated classic cut or something curly, funky and
avant-garde: she will pour her passion and technique into the look you envision.
